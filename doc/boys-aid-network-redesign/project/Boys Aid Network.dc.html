Our Mission

Raising purpose-driven young men

Boys Aid Network is a boy-child advocacy organisation focused on teaching, mentoring and helping the boychild find himself, enjoy inclusion, live a godly life and contribute to societal development.

In 2025 we rebranded from Boy-King International to Boys Aid Network, sharpening our focus on advocacy, mentorship and community support for boys across Nigeria and Africa.

Founder, Boys Aid Network
Olufunke Oyinlola
Boys Aid Network

On July 19, 2025, a dream I had carried for years finally became reality.

For a long time, I watched the boychild grow up in a world that too often overlooked him — assumed to be strong, expected to cope alone, rarely asked how he was really doing. I knew that if no one stepped in to teach, mentor and simply stand with these boys, we would keep losing them to the streets, to crime, and to quiet despair.

That conviction became Boy-King International, and in 2025 it grew into Boys Aid Network — a renewed, sharper mission to help every boy find himself, enjoy inclusion, live a godly life and contribute to society. What began as one person's burden is now a growing network of volunteers, mentors and partners who believe the same thing: boys matter, and their future is worth fighting for.

To everyone who has prayed, given, volunteered or simply believed — thank you. This is only the beginning. Together, we are empowering boys for a better tomorrow.

— Founder, Boys Aid Network
